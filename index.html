THE FOUNDER'S JOURNEY

Living with Impermanence

My journey with Anicha began with a profound realization of Pali impermanence (Anicca)—that life cannot be trapped in rigid corporate templates or digital distraction. I created Anicha to build a living ecosystem where media is honest, apparel grounds the body, mindfulness tools belong to the community, and physical retreats bring us back together off-screen.

🎓 Why Am I Qualified?

  • Decade of Mindful Practice: Deep immersive background in Pali mindfulness, Vipassana retreats, and somatic yoga.
  • Conscious Entrepreneurship: Experienced in creating authentic brands, ethical media storytelling, and non-exploitative community platforms.
  • Facilitation & Space Holding: Proven experience curating intimate offline gatherings, contemplative workshops, and sanctuary spaces.
